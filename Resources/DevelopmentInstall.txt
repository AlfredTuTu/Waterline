Waterline — development build / 开发测试版本

This image is locally ad-hoc signed. It is not a notarized public release.
Provider and native acceptance remains incomplete; see the repository's ACCEPTANCE.md.

For a local installation check, quit an existing Waterline instance before copying Waterline.app
to Applications. Preserve an existing installation if you need to compare or roll back. Eject this
image, then open the copied app. Follow macOS's normal approval flow if offered; do not disable
Gatekeeper or remove quarantine to make a failed trust check appear successful.

Daily use: the island starts compact, showing one selected account logo and its remaining allowance.
Hover does not enlarge it. Click to open details; moving away or switching apps dismisses the panel.
The pin menu selects the daily account; it does not keep the panel open. The Waterline menu in the panel contains
Collapse island, Balance history and Quit Waterline; Quit is also in the menu-bar wave icon menu.
Balance history offers 7/30/90 days in original currencies. It is not a spending or API-value report.

Antigravity: enable its optional source only after opening and signing in to the installed CLI.
Waterline reads that running local service; it does not launch the CLI or copy its tokens.
Qwen/Bailian quota lookup is not yet supported.

Account settings and usage data are stored in ~/Library/Application Support/Waterline.
Manual keys are stored in Waterline's own Keychain service. Removing the app does not remove those
settings or keys. Before uninstalling, turn off launch at login and notifications, remove manual
accounts in Settings, and resolve any reported key-cleanup failures. Other tools' logins are unchanged.

此镜像仅使用本地临时签名，不是已公证的公开发行版本。
服务商接入和原生功能验收尚未全部完成，请查阅仓库中的ACCEPTANCE.md。

本地安装检查：先退出正在运行的Waterline，将Waterline.app复制到Applications。
如需对比或回退，请保留原有安装。推出此镜像后，再打开已复制的应用。
如macOS提供正常授权流程，请按系统提示操作；不要关闭Gatekeeper或移除隔离标记来绕过检查。

日常使用：最小灵动岛显示一个选定账号的图标和剩余额度，悬停不放大。点击打开详情，
移开鼠标或切换应用会收起；图钉用于选择日常账号，不会保持展开。面板底部Waterline菜单包含“收起灵动岛”“余额记录”和
“退出Waterline”；菜单栏水波纹图标中也有退出入口。余额记录可选7／30／90天，保留
原始币种，不代表实际消费或API等价估算。

Antigravity：先打开已安装的CLI并登录，再启用可选来源。Waterline只读取运行中的本地
服务，不会启动CLI或复制Token。Qwen／百炼额度查询尚未接入。

账号设置和用量数据保存在~/Library/Application Support/Waterline中，手动密钥保存在
Waterline专属钥匙串服务中。删除应用不会同时删除这些设置和密钥。卸载前请关闭登录启动和
用量提醒，在设置中移除手动账号，并处理尚未完成的密钥清理。其他工具的登录信息不受影响。
